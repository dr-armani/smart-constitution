# The Interim Constitution for the Transition Period
**Designed & Developed by** Daniel Armani ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dr-armani) ••• [Website](https://www.blockcenter.org/ceo))

An **Interim Constitution designed for implementation as a smart contract ("Smart Constitution")**.

This framework governs the formation, operation, accountability, and dissolution of a Transitional Government (**TG**) during a democratic transition.

## Implementation Categories

Each provision is classified by its dominant implementation mode:

- **`[A] On-chain executable`** — can be automatically enforced by smart-contract logic.
- **`[B] Cryptographically verifiable off-chain`** — requires real-world action or data, but compliance can be authenticated and recorded on-chain.
- **`[C] Human/legal judgment`** — requires institutional, legal, or human interpretation and cannot be reliably enforced by code alone.

## i. Formation of the Transitional Government

### i.1 `[A]`
The TG shall consist of **100 Members**.

### i.2 `[B]`
Initially, three trusted **Neutral Registrars** are appointed by the international coalition (Section vi) to select a representative random sample of the population to vote.

### i.3 `[B]`
Within **4 weeks** of this appointment, the Neutral Registrars shall randomly select **1,200 citizens** to vote anonymously and elect Members on a blockchain.

### i.4 `[B]`
The official birth dates of the 1,200 randomly selected voters shall fall between **January 1957 and December 2006 (inclusive)**, spanning 50 years (600 months) in total.

There shall be exactly two voters born in each of these 600 months:

- one biologically male at birth; and
- one biologically female at birth.

This ensures a uniform distribution of age and gender, with exactly:

- **600 biologically male voters**, and
- **600 biologically female voters**.

This maximizes diversity while limiting the choices available to the Neutral Registrars.

### i.5 `[A]`
During this 4-week period, except for the Neutral Registrars, any person may register on the blockchain as a candidate for Membership in the TG.

No entity shall exclude or vet any candidate; eligibility shall be determined solely by the voters.

### i.6 `[A]`
If, by day 28, the Neutral Registrars have selected 1,200 voters and at least **200 candidates** are registered, the campaign period shall commence and last for **14 days**.

### i.7 `[A]`
If the thresholds for candidates or voters are not met by day 28, the registration period shall be extended until both are met.

Once met, the campaign period shall commence and last for **14 days**.

### i.8 `[A]`
On the first day after the 14-day campaign period, voters may vote in an **open, inclusive, and free election**.

The election shall last **24 hours**.

### i.9 `[A]`
Every voter may vote for one or multiple candidates on the blockchain using **Approval Voting** to elect one hundred (100) Members of the TG.

### i.10 `[A]`
The one hundred (100) candidates with the most approval votes shall become Members of the TG.

### i.11 `[A]`
The Member receiving the highest number of votes shall serve as the leader of the TG for the first **two (2) weeks**.

### i.12 `[A]`
Every two weeks, leadership shall rotate to the Member with the next highest vote count.

In case of a tie, the Member who declared candidacy earliest shall become leader first.

### i.13 `[A]`
No person may serve as leader more than once or for over **two weeks**.

### i.14 `[C]`
The leader is responsible for:

- coordinating among Members;
- organizing Member meetings; and
- reporting progress to the public.

## ii. Decisions of the Transitional Government

### ii.1 `[A]`
All votes, decisions, and financial transactions of TG Members shall be recorded publicly on a blockchain.

### ii.2 `[C]`
All records and intelligence of the former regime shall be preserved and published online within a maximum of **20 weeks**.

No information is considered confidential, and the destruction of any record is a crime.

### ii.3 `[A]`
The TG shall not amend or override any part of this Interim Constitution.

### ii.4 `[A]`
Any Member may submit a **Proposal** concerning:

- policies;
- legislation;
- decisions; or
- financial actions.

### ii.5 `[A]`
A Proposal shall be ratified upon receiving the approval of **sixty (60) Members**.

### ii.6 `[A]`
A Proposal may be withdrawn by its proposer prior to ratification.

### ii.7 `[A]`
A Proposal shall remain under consideration until:

- ratified; or
- withdrawn by the proposing Member.

Members may use **Artificial Intelligence** to evaluate Proposals.

### ii.8 `[A]`
Each Member may have only **one Proposal under consideration at a time** and may not submit a new Proposal until the prior Proposal is ratified or withdrawn.

### ii.9 `[C]`
The TG may authorize Artificial Intelligence systems to adjudicate legal cases.

Such decisions shall be subject to appeal as if rendered by a human judge, and the TG shall establish rules governing their:

- transparency;
- auditability; and
- operating parameters.

## iii. Duties of the Transitional Government

The TG shall:

### iii.1 `[C]`
Be the sovereign governing authority and maintain stability, law, and order throughout the transition period.

### iii.2 `[C]`
Supervise all government institutions and exercise control over:

- the armed forces;
- law enforcement; and
- the judiciary

during the transition period.

### iii.3 `[C]`
Comply with applicable international human rights treaties, including the **International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR)**.

The **Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR)** shall guide interpretation and implementation.

### iii.4 `[A]`
Enable each Member to submit **one constitutional draft** on the blockchain within **ten (10) weeks** of formation.

### iii.5 `[A]`
Make all submitted constitutional drafts publicly available for voting.

### iii.6 `[B]`
Enable every citizen aged **eighteen (18) or older** to vote in the referendum for one or multiple constitutional drafts on the blockchain.

### iii.7 `[B]`
Recruit enough registrars to ensure every eligible voter is registered by **two independent registrars**.

### iii.8 `[A]`
Conduct a national constitutional referendum on the blockchain using **Approval Voting** within **fourteen (14) weeks** of formation.

### iii.9 `[A]`
Ratify the constitutional draft receiving the highest number of approval votes as the **Constitution of the country**.

### iii.10 `[B]`
Hold national elections in accordance with the newly ratified Constitution to establish the new government within **eight (8) weeks** of the referendum.

### iii.11 `[B]`
Dissolve itself and transfer all power to the newly elected government within **two (2) weeks** after the national elections.

## iv. Failure and Replacement of the Transitional Government

If the TG fails to fulfill its duties within **24 weeks**:

### iv.1 `[B]`
Three new Neutral Registrars shall be appointed to restart the process.

### iv.2 `[B]`
The new Neutral Registrars shall randomly select **1,200 new citizens** to vote for candidates.

### iv.3 `[A]`
The 1,200 newly selected citizens shall elect **one hundred (100) new Members** in an open, inclusive, and free election.

### iv.4 `[A]`
The existing TG shall be dissolved and replaced by the newly formed TG composed of one hundred (100) new Members.

### iv.5 `[A]`
If the constitutional referendum has not yet been held, the new TG shall proceed to hold the referendum following Section iii.

### iv.6 `[A]`
If a constitutional referendum has already been held, the new TG shall hold a new referendum, and its result shall supersede all prior outcomes.

## v. Restrictions on TG Members

Members of the TG shall **NOT**:

### v.1 `[B]`
Run for office or participate in any election for the new government.

### v.2 `[B]`
Accept any official or unofficial government position for **three (3) years**, thereby ensuring election integrity and avoiding conflicts of interest.

## vi. International Coalition

A coalition of international organizations, such as the **United Nations**, shall:

### vi.1 `[B]`
Appoint three Neutral Registrars prior to the commencement of the process.

### vi.2 `[B]`
Ensure the random selection of **1,200 voters** from the citizenry.

### vi.3 `[B]`
Appoint:

- three (3) international military commanders; and
- two (2) local military commanders

to command the armed forces and maintain public order until the TG is formed.

### vi.4 `[C]`
Ensure that the appointed commanders shall have no authority to:

- adjudicate;
- sentence; or
- execute any person.

They may detain suspects to be processed by forthcoming judicial authorities.

### vi.5 `[B]`
Terminate the authority of the appointed commanders immediately upon formation of the TG.

### vi.6 `[C]`
Oversee elections and the constitutional referendum to ensure their integrity and transparency.

### vi.7 `[C]`
Monitor compliance with:

- this Interim Constitution; and
- applicable international human rights treaties, including the ICCPR.

## Smart Constitution Architecture

The constitutional framework is intended to operate through three interacting layers:

| Layer | Function |
|---|---|
| **Smart Contract Layer** | Executes deterministic constitutional rules, elections, voting, proposal ratification, deadlines, leadership rotation, and state transitions. |
| **Identity / Oracle / Registrar Layer** | Verifies citizenship, age, voter eligibility, registrar attestations, appointments, and other real-world facts. |
| **Institutional / Legal Layer** | Handles human-rights interpretation, public order, judicial functions, institutional supervision, and other matters requiring legal or human judgment. |

## Governance Model

The system combines:

- blockchain-based elections;
- approval voting;
- transparent legislative voting;
- public financial records;
- rotating executive coordination;
- constitutional referenda;
- cryptographic voter verification;
- institutional checks;
- AI-assisted proposal evaluation; and
- potentially AI-assisted adjudication subject to appeal.

The Interim Constitution is designed to remain **non-amendable by the TG** and to terminate automatically upon the successful establishment of the permanent constitutional government.
